Digital Edge

Proprietary Phygital Lending Architecture

Eliminating friction with paperless single-use links, automated e-KYC, multi-lender eligibility matrix, and real-time commission disbursements.

01

Instant Secure Link Generation

The branch executive or advisor generates a single-use secure link. Borrower uploads documents from their phone with zero app download or login barriers.

02

Algorithmic Document OCR & Credit Check

Instant verification of PAN, Aadhaar, ITRs, and GST returns. Integrated Account Aggregator (AA) parses bank statements and calculates real-time FOIR.

03

Multi-Lender Policy Matching

Our rule matrix scans 75+ bank credit policies to route the file directly to the bank with the highest approval probability and lowest rate of interest.

04

In-Principle Sanction & Dedicated Processing

Central Processing Unit (CPU) coordinates with bank credit managers for fast-track property valuation, legal search, and final sanction letter issue.

05

Disbursement & Instant Payout Credit

Funds disbursed to borrower’s account. Advisor commission ledger is credited transparently in real time with complete invoice generation.
